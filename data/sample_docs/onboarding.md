# Engineer Onboarding

## First Week

New engineers receive a laptop with the standard image already enrolled in device management.
On day one they are assigned an onboarding buddy from their team, who is their first point of
contact for questions for the first six weeks. By the end of the first week a new engineer should
have the development environment running and a small change merged to production.

## First Month

In the first month new engineers complete the security awareness training, the incident response
training, and read the runbooks for their team's services. They shadow one on-call week as an
observer before joining the rotation; nobody joins the on-call rotation within their first 8 weeks.

## Access

Access is requested through the access broker using role templates, so a new backend engineer
gets the same baseline entitlements as their peers without custom tickets.

## Feedback

The onboarding checklist is owned by the developer experience team. New engineers are asked for
feedback at 30 and 90 days, and every friction point reported is either fixed or explained in the
checklist.
