# Privacy and Data Subject Requests

## Data Subject Requests

Customers may request a copy of their personal data or its deletion. Requests are logged in the
privacy portal and must be completed within 30 days of receipt. Identity is verified before any
data is released; a request from an address not on the account is never fulfilled directly.

Deletion requests remove personal data from production systems within 30 days. Backups age out
under the normal retention schedule rather than being edited, and a restored backup re-applies the
deletion log before it serves traffic.

## Data Protection Officer

The Data Protection Officer is reachable at privacy@company.example and must be consulted before
any new processing of personal data for a purpose not covered by the privacy notice.

## Privacy Reviews

New features that collect a new category of personal data require a privacy impact assessment
before launch. The assessment records the purpose, the retention period, and who can access the
data.

## Sub-processors

Vendors that process customer personal data are listed publicly as sub-processors. Customers are
notified 30 days before a new sub-processor begins processing their data.
